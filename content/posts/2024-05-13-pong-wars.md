I've published my Godot re-creation of [Pong Wars by Koen van Gilst](https://pong-wars.koenvangilst.nl/) on [GitHub](https://rosskarchner.github.io/pong-wars-godot/) (and embedded it below!) It's not exactly a *game*-- more of a "visual toy". I did add some fun extras:

- left click a block to instantly flip it's color
- right click anywhere to flip ALL of the blocks.
