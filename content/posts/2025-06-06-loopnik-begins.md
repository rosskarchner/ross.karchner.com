Some progress on the ["beat game" remake](https://ross.karchner.com/2025/06/04/from-so-bad-its-good.html) (for the moment, called "Loopnik"). It's much easier now that I have a better idea of what I'm doing and how to structure things. Aside from level advancement, this does everything the original does, with half the lines of code.

The "graphics" are kind of charmless and generic right now, but they're placeholders. The donut is from Kenney.
