So, instead of [putting the SpinDoc level editor aside](https://ross.karchner.com/2025/12/30/thinking-about-spindoc-since-i.html), I dug in and now the thing generally works. I even got online level sharing to work: it spits out a URL that anyone can use to play your level. That's something I've been day-dreaming about for over a year.
