["The Last Stan" is live on itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gamedevtv-jam-2024/rate/2748401) and submitted to the gamedev.tv jam. It's a platformer, with typing. 

It's not my favorite thing I've made, but I will say that it's got some charm (a good deal of which comes from the [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/) art and background music by [Seth Makes Sounds](https://freesound.org/people/Seth_Makes_Sounds/sounds/722430/)).
