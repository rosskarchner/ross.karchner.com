- "rings" (aka, dots you can only use once)
- enemy wands that can move to appropriately colored dots

In November, I'll focus on the [GitHub Game Off](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2024). When I get back to SpinDoc after that, it'll be more level design, some original mechanics I've been thinking off, and polish.
