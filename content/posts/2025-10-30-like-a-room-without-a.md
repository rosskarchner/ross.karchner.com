I was playing [Glypha](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2318420/Glypha_Vintage/) the other day, and wondered if an  "endless Joust" game would be fun-- instead of discrete levels, you keep proceeding upward.  It turns out, [Joust Revival](https://dlgcreations.itch.io/joust-revival) is exactly that. I can't get it to work with Wine, though, and if a game isn't playable on Linux, does it *really* exist?

Either way, *Revival* is otherwise a re-creation of the classic arcade Joust game. I have something different in mind, but definitely with the DNA of Joust. Or Glypha. I'm pretty sure I played Glypha first. I think I'm going to give it a try in the upcoming [GitHub Game Off](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2025).

2025 hasn't been a productive year for making games. The job hunt and related Government uncertainty basically ate up the first seven months, and I haven't really recovered the lost momentum. I _am_ going to try to end strong with three simple jam games before the end of the year, the one described above, and two jams I've enjoyed participating with in the past: the [1-Button Jam](https://itch.io/jam/1-button-jam-2025) and the [20 Second Game Jam](https://itch.io/jam/20-second-game-jam-2025). Embrace constraints!

[Pinball Putt](https://rosskarchner.itch.io/pinball-putt) was my entry in the 2024 1 Button Jam, and [twenty thousand milliseconds](https://rosskarchner.itch.io/twenty-thousand-milliseconds) my entry in the 2023 20 Second Game Jam.
