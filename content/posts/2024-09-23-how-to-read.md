Finished reading: [How to Read Superhero Comics and why](https://micro.blog/books/9780826414182) by Geoff Klock 📚

This was more academic than I was expecting (full of words like 'misprision' and 'kenosis'), but Klock makes a convincing case that the books he writes about exist in conversation with each other, the comics of past eras, and criticism like [Seduction of the Innocent](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seduction_of_the_Innocent). 

I'm not sure if that interplay is supposed to answer the 'why' question by itself, but it does have me somewhat intrigued-- at least enough to check out some books I've missed (like Marvels, Astro City, Kingdom Come, America's Best Comics, and Grant Morrison's JLA).
