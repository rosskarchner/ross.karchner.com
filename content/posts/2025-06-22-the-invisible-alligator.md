I'm not that proud of [The Invisible Alligator](https://rosskarchner.itch.io/the-invisible-alligator) in its current form, but the [Godot Wild Jam 82](https://itch.io/jam/godot-wild-jam-82) deadline is here, and it's nice to ship a game after almost 6 months of not doing much gamedev.

It could use more levels, sound and music, animation, more NPC variety, and more effort spent on graphics. The core gameplay isn't that _fun_ yet, but Benjamin calls it "satisfying", so that's something.

I started out thinking about it as a 2D version of Hitman Go, with animals. It ended up in a place that (if built out more) would feel a bit like [Hoplite](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoplite_(video_game)). 

Maybe I'll keep tinkering, towards a more polished post-jam version.
