I'm signed up for [Godot Wild Jam #66](https://itch.io/jam/godot-wild-jam-66), with a theme of "Escape"-- which is pretty straightforward, as far as game jam themes go. I do think it's quite cool that GWJ has accessibility as a rating category, and I'm going to try to see how much of the [accessibility guidelines](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_KRsj_tC5YynemDBMt-v6YVzEnWkqESF/view) I can meet.

The jam goes for nine days. My current thinking on timing is: have something basically working by early next week, then use the rest of the time to polish the graphics, UI, and audio.
