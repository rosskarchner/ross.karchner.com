Today, with my family, I watched the first episode of [Human Footprint](https://www.pbs.org/show/human-footprint/), which is about invasive species. It's full of US government employees (I noticed USGS, BLM, and Army Corps of Engineers) doing important science and engineering: keeping asian carp out of the great lakes, studying pythons in the everglades, and managing the wild horse population in the west.

I had to wonder, after these last few weeks... do these people still have jobs? What will happen without them?
