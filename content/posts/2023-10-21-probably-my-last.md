[The results are in](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/54/dont-get-crushed), and... it's nothing to brag about. I do like that innovation is my strongest category again.
 
<img src="/uploads/2023/screenshot-from-2023-10-21-17-10-29.png" width="549" height="274" alt="rankings for my game 'Don't Get Crushed' in Ludum Dare 54">

I don't know exactly how many entrees I'm ranked against. The [stats](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/54/stats) say that there were 1597 entries in the "jam" competition, and 66% of all entries ("jam", and a separate competition, "compo") met the ratings threshold for getting ranked, so maybe I'm ranked against 1067 or so "jam" entries? That puts me in the top half for most categories, and not far from it on the Overall. In a competition that includes professionals, hobbyists, and teams, I feel OK about that.
