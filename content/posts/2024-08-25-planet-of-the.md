This morning I was thinking back on some videos I've watched (like [this](https://youtu.be/cWcJejr3Fl4?si=vCocSi_EQ1tEIhXJ)) about abandoned NFT games, and it occurred to me that such a virtual world might be a compelling setting for a new game. 

A world of half-built and/or I'll-conceived villas and islands, bugs (exploitable?) that will never be fixed, bad algorithmic art, a massively deflated in-game currency. Everything you see was put there by VC, whales, crypto true believers, speculators, scammers and dupes.

Any random [Molly White blog post](https://www.web3isgoinggreat.com/) could be a plot line.
